How this is worked out

Each stage starts from a typical success rate for UK engineering placement schemes, which shortlist hard: about 8% at application, 10% when an online assessment is next, 15% while awaiting an outcome, 22% at video interview, 40% at assessment centre. Every round you've already cleared multiplies that by 1.3, because each one removes a big share of the field. Use the − and + buttons to record rounds cleared, and change the status in the list above as things move. The overall figure is the chance that at least one comes through. These are estimates to help you prioritise, not real odds.

Add your Canvas calendar

Your planner reads deadlines from your private Canvas calendar feed. You only need to do this once.

  1. On a computer, open Canvas and click Calendar in the left-hand menu.
  2. At the bottom of the right-hand column, click Calendar Feed.
  3. Copy the link it shows. It starts with https:// and ends in .ics.

The link is stored only in your own Google account. Anyone who has it can see your Canvas calendar, so keep it private.
