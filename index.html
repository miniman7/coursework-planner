Add your Canvas calendar

Your planner reads deadlines from your private Canvas calendar feed. You only need to do this once.

  1. On a computer, open Canvas and click Calendar in the left-hand menu.
  2. At the bottom of the right-hand column, click Calendar Feed.
  3. Copy the link it shows. It starts with https:// and ends in .ics.

The link is stored only in your own Google account. Anyone who has it can see your Canvas calendar, so keep it private.
