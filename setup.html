Set up your Coursework Planner

Your Canvas deadlines in one tidy app, with statuses, notes, a workload chart and reminder emails a week, three days and a day before each deadline.

FreeRuns on your own free Google account.
PrivateYour coursework stays in your Google Drive. Nobody else can see it.
About 5 minutesDo it on a laptop. Your phone is only needed at the end.

You'll create a small script in your Google account. It keeps your list in a Google Sheet, checks Canvas every morning and emails you reminders. Google shows a few warnings along the way because the script is yours rather than a published app. This guide tells you what to click at each one.

  1. Copy the script

    This copies the planner's script so you can paste it into your Google account in the next step.

  2. Paste it into a new Apps Script project

    IN APPS SCRIPT

    • Open script.new. Sign in with Google if it asks.
    • Click in the code area, press Ctrl+A (⌘+A on Mac) to select what's there, then Ctrl+V (⌘+V) to paste.
    • Click Untitled project at the top and rename it Coursework Planner.
    • Click the Save icon (💾).

  3. Deploy it as a web app

    IN APPS SCRIPT

    • Click Deploy (top right), then New deployment.
    • Click the gear ⚙ next to Select type and choose Web app.
    • Leave Execute as: Me. Change Who has access to Anyone.
    • Click Deploy, then Authorise access, and pick your Google account.
    • Google says "Google hasn't verified this app". Click Advanced, then Go to Coursework Planner (unsafe), then Allow.
    • Copy the Web app URL it shows (it ends in /exec), then click Done.

    "Anyone" only means your phone can reach it without a Google sign-in. Every request needs your private access key from the next step, so nobody else can read or change your data.

  4. Run setup

    IN APPS SCRIPT

    • In the toolbar, choose setup from the function dropdown (it may say myFunction or doGet), then click Run. If Google asks for permission again, click Allow.
    • The Execution log opens at the bottom. It shows YOUR ACCESS KEY, and usually a ready-made phone link too.

    This creates a Google Sheet called "Coursework Planner" in your Drive and schedules the morning check.

  5. Get the app on your phone

    If the log showed a phone link, send it to yourself privately (for example in an email to yourself) and open it on your phone. Otherwise, paste your two details here to make the link and a QR code. This happens only on this page and nothing is sent anywhere.

    Scan this with your phone's camera, or copy the link to open it elsewhere. Keep it private, because it unlocks your planner.

    Open on this device

  6. Add your Canvas calendar

    IN THE PLANNER

    The first time it opens, the planner asks for your Canvas calendar link:

    • On a computer, open Canvas and click Calendar in the left-hand menu.
    • At the bottom of the right-hand column, click Calendar Feed and copy the link (it ends in .ics).
    • Paste it into the planner and tap Load my assignments.

    Then add the planner to your home screen. On iPhone (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android (Chrome): ⋮ → Install app.

    To check reminders work, tap Sync → Send test reminder.

Questions

Who can see my assignments?

Only you. They're stored in a Google Sheet in your own Drive, and your Canvas link is stored in your own script. The person who shared this app can't see any of it. The app's code is public, but it contains no one's data.

Why does Google say the app is unsafe?

Google shows that warning for any script that isn't published on its marketplace, including ones you make yourself. The script only edits its own Sheet, reads your Canvas calendar feed, and sends emails to you.

When do reminders arrive?

Around 7am UK time, 7, 3 and 1 days before each deadline and on the day, plus up to 3 days after if you miss one. Nothing is sent on days with nothing to report. Assignments you mark Submitted or Hidden don't get reminders.

How do I use it on another device?

Open the same phone link on that device, or come back to step 5 and make the link again.

How do I stop it?

Open your Coursework Planner project at script.google.com and delete it, or open Triggers (the alarm clock icon) and delete the dailyJob trigger. You can also delete the "Coursework Planner" sheet from your Drive.
